Account Data Deletion Request

Warning: Account deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety guidelines, you have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all associated personal data from our systems.

What data gets deleted?

  • Your user profile (username, avatar, bio)
  • Your movie vault (watchlist, favorites, watched history)
  • Your social connections (friends list, recommendations, sharing history)
  • Your authentication data

How to delete your account:

  1. Open the Movie Tracker app on your device.
  2. Tap the hamburger menu in the top left corner.
  3. Tap the "Delete Account" button at the bottom of the menu in red.
  4. Confirm your decision. Your data will be deleted instantly.

If you no longer have the app installed and would like us to manually purge your data from our database, please send an email request to the developer using the email address associated with your account.
